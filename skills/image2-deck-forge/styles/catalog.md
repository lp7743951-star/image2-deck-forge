# Style Catalog

Choose one Style Lock for an entire deck. Do not blend styles unless the user explicitly requests a hybrid.

| Style | Best for | Signature |
|---|---|---|
| [Terminal Tech Magazine](terminal-tech-magazine.md) | AI, developer tools, product architecture, technical workflows | Black terminal surfaces, mint/cyan signals, sparse HUD geometry |
| [Impact Grid Editorial](impact-grid-editorial.md) | Strategy, institutions, consulting, research, general impact | Warm paper, black editorial grid, deep teal documentary panels |
| [Climate Impact Editorial Grid](climate-impact-editorial-grid.md) | Climate, ESG, sustainability, environmental evidence | Cool paper, muted field photography, restrained environmental palette |
| [French Editorial Commerce](french-editorial-commerce.md) | Fashion, premium commerce, lifestyle, beauty, brand storytelling | Cream paper, condensed display type, serif accents, film-grain imagery |
| [Aubergine Semantic Future](aubergine-semantic-future.md) | AI futures, transformation, systems thinking, conceptual narratives | Deep plum field, ivory/gold typography, meaning-led imagery |
| [Orbit Flow Keynote · 星环流光](orbit-flow-keynote.md) | AI workshops, product keynotes, business frameworks, portrait information posters | White field, cropped blue orbit, violet-orange flow, bold Chinese type, numbered modules, navy conclusion band |

## Selection heuristic

- Use Terminal Tech when the subject is a system, tool, model, workflow, or infrastructure.
- Use Impact Grid for broad institutional or strategic evidence.
- Use Climate Impact only when environmental context is central, not merely decorative.
- Use French Editorial Commerce when desirability, craft, taste, or product storytelling matters.
- Use Aubergine Semantic Future when the deck needs conceptual depth and future-facing drama without generic robot imagery.
- Use Orbit Flow for clear Chinese business communication with a bright technology motif. Match aliases `Orbit Flow`, `星环流光`, `白底星环`, and `紫橙流光` to `orbit-flow-keynote.md`. Avoid dense financial reports, academic prose, and formal legal documents. Do not turn it into Terminal Tech's dark HUD or Aubergine's plum photographic world.
