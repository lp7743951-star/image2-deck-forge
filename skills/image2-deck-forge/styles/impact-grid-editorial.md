# Impact Grid Editorial

## Identity

A rigorous institutional editorial system that turns evidence into a clear public argument.

## Use

Best for strategy, consulting, public institutions, research, impact reporting, and cross-domain analysis. It is broader and warmer than the climate-specific variant.

## Visual DNA

- Typography: condensed sans for claims, neutral sans for body and labels.
- Palette: warm paper `#F4F2EF`, carbon `#111111`, charcoal `#2B2F2E`, deep teal `#183B3D`, stone `#D8D6D0`, sage `#8FAE9A`, muted brass `#C7A46A`.
- Grid: hard editorial modules, visible column logic, thin black rules, deliberate asymmetry.
- Containers and borders: square rectangles and flat color blocks; no rounded cards and no outer frame.
- Text density: moderate but hierarchical; one headline, one evidence line, one to three labels.
- Image direction: documentary people, workplaces, public systems, materials, infrastructure.
- Negative rules: no glossy startup gradients, no floating UI cards, no playful icons, no climate symbolism unless the subject requires it.

## Style Lock

Keep warm paper, black grid rules, deep teal blocks, documentary evidence, square geometry, and controlled asymmetry. Typography, palette, grid, containers, text density, image direction, and negative rules must remain stable across the deck.

## Page Behavior

- Cover: bold claim aligned to an off-center documentary crop.
- Evidence: one image, one statistic, and one explanatory sentence.
- Framework: two to four square modules linked by rules.
- Comparison: opposing columns with a shared baseline.
- Closing: oversized conclusion on paper with one narrow image strip.

## Prompt Prefix

Create a premium 16:9 institutional editorial slide on warm paper. Use a strict black grid, square deep-teal modules, condensed headline typography, and one authentic documentary image. Make the argument obvious at thumbnail size; avoid rounded cards and decorative effects.

## Quality Gate

Confirm the hierarchy is unmistakable, the image is evidentiary rather than decorative, all geometry is square, the palette remains warm and institutional, and no environmental cues appear without semantic reason.
