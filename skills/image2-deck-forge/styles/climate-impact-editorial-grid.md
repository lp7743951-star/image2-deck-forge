# Climate Impact Editorial Grid

## Identity

A sober evidence-led editorial system for environmental change, resilience, and collective action.

## Use

Best for climate, ESG, sustainability, energy transition, conservation, environmental policy, and field research. Do not use it as generic green decoration.

## Visual DNA

- Typography: authoritative condensed sans for claims; neutral sans for notes and evidence.
- Palette: cool paper `#F4F2EE`, deep ink `#101817`, black `#161616`, field gray `#77736B`, mineral beige `#C9C3B8`, sea-glass teal `#5FAF9A`, forest teal `#183B3A`.
- Grid: Swiss editorial grid with calm whitespace, thin rules, and evidence-first sequencing.
- Containers and borders: flat rectangular panels and image crops; no rounded cards, no outer frame.
- Text density: restrained; one claim, one datum, and one implication.
- Image direction: low-saturation field photography of water, land, air, materials, energy, communities, and infrastructure.
- Negative rules: no leaf icons, no glowing planet, no greenwashing imagery, no oversaturated nature, no generic corporate handshake.

## Style Lock

Keep cool paper, muted environmental documentary imagery, sea-glass accents, thin black grid rules, square panels, and an evidence-first rhythm. Typography, palette, grid, containers, text density, image direction, and negative rules must remain stable across the deck.

## Page Behavior

- Cover: environmental field image with a compact but forceful claim.
- Evidence: one measured phenomenon, one contextual image, one consequence.
- Map or system: simplified geography or flow with muted environmental colors.
- Scenario: two or three restrained columns; avoid alarmist spectacle.
- Closing: human-scale landscape or infrastructure image plus one actionable statement.

## Prompt Prefix

Create a premium 16:9 climate editorial slide on cool paper. Use a strict Swiss grid, muted sea-glass teal, low-saturation environmental documentary photography, thin rules, and one evidence-led claim. Avoid green cliches, glossy cards, and decorative nature motifs.

## Quality Gate

Confirm the environmental image directly supports the claim, the palette is muted, the grid is calm, the evidence is readable, and nothing implies sustainability through symbolism alone.
