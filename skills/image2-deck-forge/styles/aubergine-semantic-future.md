# Aubergine Semantic Future

## Identity

A future-facing editorial world where every image is selected for meaning, not for generic AI spectacle.

## Use

Best for AI futures, transformation, systems thinking, emerging technology, conceptual narratives, and strategic change. This is the signature new style.

## Visual DNA

- Typography: oversized modern grotesk for claims, refined serif or light grotesk for reflective support.
- Palette: aubergine `#2A1D30`, deep plum `#33223A`, violet ink `#402B47`, night `#1F1624`, dusty plum `#513757`, ivory `#F7F2E8`, pale gold `#E6D6A7`, lavender gray `#D8C7DE`, lilac `#C9ADD8`, moss `#6F8C65`, pale cyan `#9FD5E2`, ember `#D4682B`.
- Grid: spacious editorial geometry with one large claim and one semantic image anchor.
- Containers and borders: mostly open field; occasional translucent plum plane; no light central card, no HUD, no full outer frame.
- Text density: very low; one claim, one thought, one small label if needed.
- Image direction: choose the image from the page thesis: passages, materials, human gestures, branching structures, landscapes, adaptive architecture, living systems, or machines only when truly relevant.
- Negative rules: no generic robot, no floating brain, no circuit-face portrait, no irrelevant hand-machine handshake, no purple neon sci-fi, no repeated image trope.

## Style Lock

Keep a deep plum field, ivory and pale-gold typography, spacious composition, and one thesis-driven image. Typography, palette, grid, containers, text density, image direction, and negative rules must remain stable. Robot or mechanical-hand imagery should occupy no more than 20 percent of a deck by default, and the same image family should not repeat on more than two consecutive slides.

## Page Behavior

- Cover: monumental claim with one poetic but semantically exact image.
- Thesis: text-led composition with a narrow image aperture.
- System: one branching or layered structure, not a dashboard.
- Tension: opposing image and statement with deliberate empty space.
- Closing: a calm future-facing image and one memorable line.

## Prompt Prefix

Create a premium 16:9 future editorial slide on a deep aubergine field. Use oversized ivory typography, restrained pale-gold accents, generous negative space, and one visually arresting image chosen from the exact meaning of the claim. Avoid generic AI imagery, HUD graphics, light central cards, and decorative futurism.

## Quality Gate

Confirm the image cannot be swapped for an unrelated futuristic picture without weakening the argument, text is minimal and legible, the plum field remains dominant, and repeated robot or mechanical-hand imagery is absent.
