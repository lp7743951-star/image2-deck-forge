# French Editorial Commerce

## Identity

A cinematic French commerce magazine: cultured, tactile, desirable, and quietly commercial.

## Use

Best for fashion, premium retail, beauty, food, lifestyle, hospitality, craftsmanship, and brand storytelling. Avoid technical architecture and institutional reporting.

## Visual DNA

- Typography: narrow condensed display sans for headlines, elegant serif for editorial contrast, small neutral sans for commerce details.
- Palette: ivory `#F4EFE7`, cream `#EFE4D6`, sand `#E3D2BD`, espresso `#18120E`, caramel `#6B3F2A`, burgundy `#6F1F24`, olive `#4E5A32`, taupe `#B9A693`.
- Grid: fashion-editorial asymmetry, cropped columns, deliberate negative space.
- Containers and borders: paper blocks, image crops, occasional hairline rule; avoid interface cards and outer frames.
- Text density: low to moderate; headline as object, minimal supporting copy.
- Image direction: premium still life, fashion, hands, materials, interiors, food, film grain, directional daylight.
- Negative rules: no tech HUD, no glossy marketplace UI, no cheap luxury cliches, no gold foil overload, no collage clutter.

## Style Lock

Keep cream paper, caramel and burgundy accents, condensed display type, serif contrast, tactile film-grain imagery, and editorial asymmetry. Typography, palette, grid, containers, text density, image direction, and negative rules must remain stable across the deck.

## Page Behavior

- Cover: one iconic image crop with a large condensed title.
- Product story: tactile close-up, short serif note, one commerce cue.
- Collection: two or three images with disciplined spacing, never a busy catalog grid.
- Quote: generous cream space with a portrait or material detail.
- Closing: quiet aspirational image and a short invitation.

## Prompt Prefix

Create a premium 16:9 French commerce editorial slide on cream paper. Use a large condensed headline, refined serif accents, caramel, burgundy, and olive notes, one tactile film-grain image, and sophisticated negative space. Avoid interface styling and obvious luxury cliches.

## Quality Gate

Confirm the slide feels editorial before commercial, the image is tactile and specific, typography carries the composition, accents are restrained, and no technology UI appears.
